# Halal Mortgage Calculator

Free, open-source tool for UK Muslims to compare Islamic Home Purchase Plans with conventional mortgages.

**[halalmortgagecalculator.org.uk](https://halalmortgagecalculator.org.uk)**

---

## Features

- Side-by-side HPP vs conventional comparison
- Live sliders for price, deposit, term and rates
- Year-by-year balance and cost charts
- Stamp duty calculator with first-time buyer relief
- Affordability checker (4.5×, 4.75×, 6.5× income)
- Overpayment calculator
- City price presets
- Shareable URLs
- Dark mode
- No tracking, works offline

---

## How it works

Islamic mortgages use **Diminishing Musharakah** to avoid interest (riba):

1. The bank co-buys the property with you
2. You pay rent on their share plus acquisition payments
3. Your ownership grows until you own 100%
